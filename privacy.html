Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 July 2026

Deskestra collects no data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, and no server of ours anywhere.

Deskestra itself never sends anything over the network. The one time a connection happens at all is when you buy or restore an instrument, and that connection is made by Apple's In-App Purchase system, not by us — see Purchases below. Everything else works permanently offline, and nothing about how you use the app is ever transmitted.

The microphone

One feature uses your microphone: the Tuner, which has to hear the note you are playing in order to measure its pitch.

  • Recording starts when the Tuner becomes the instrument on screen, and stops the moment you switch to another instrument or hide the window. The macOS recording indicator therefore reflects exactly what the app is doing.
  • The incoming audio is analysed for pitch in memory and immediately discarded. It is never written to disk, never retained, and never transmitted.
  • macOS asks for your permission the first time. If you decline, the Tuner simply reports that it has no input; every other instrument works normally.
  • You can change your mind at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

What is stored on your Mac

Deskestra remembers your own settings — the selected instrument, chosen voices, volume, theme, octave, tempo, tuner reference pitch, and which instruments you have unlocked. These live in the app's normal macOS preferences on your own machine. They are not personal data, and they are not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Instruments are unlocked through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple, not Deskestra, handles the entire transaction — including the network connection to the App Store when you buy or press Restore Purchases. We never see your name, your address, or your payment details. What reaches the app is only the fact that a given instrument has been purchased. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

Deskestra is suitable for any age. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing about a child — or anyone else — for it to collect.

Changes

If a future version of Deskestra ever needed to collect anything, this page would be updated before that version shipped, and the change would be described plainly here rather than buried.
